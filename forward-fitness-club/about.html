Our Story

From a single garage gym to a full training club

Forward Fitness Club started in 2016 as a 400 sq ft garage gym run by two coaches who were tired of watching people quit fitness because it felt intimidating or impersonal. Today we're a full facility with strength, cardio, and studio spaces — but the original goal hasn't changed: help real people build strength and confidence they keep.

Every program we run is designed and reviewed by our head coaching staff, not templated from a franchise playbook. That's what lets us adapt for beginners, competitive athletes, and everyone training around a busy life in between.
